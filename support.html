Support

Answers to the questions that come up most. If yours isn't here, write to the address at the bottom of this page.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone or iPad?

There are two ways.

  • With iCloud: on both devices, signed in to the same Apple Account, turn on Settings → iCloud → Sync with iCloud. Your agendas, journal, photos, and focus history appear on the other device after a short while.
  • With a backup file: on the old device, go to Settings → Data Management → Prepare Backup, then Export Backup and send the file to the new device (AirDrop or Files both work). On the new device, choose Import Backup and pick the file.

What is the difference between Replace and Merge when importing?

Replace All Data erases what is in the app and puts the backup in its place. Merge with Existing keeps what you have and only adds the entries that are not already there.

iCloud sync is on, but my other device doesn't show my data

  • The switch is per device. Check that Sync with iCloud is on in OmniFlow on both devices.
  • Both devices must use the same Apple Account, and iCloud must be allowed for the app in the Settings app (it is listed there as OmniFlow).
  • The line under the switch tells you what is happening: when it last synced, or whether it is waiting for a connection, a sign-in, or free iCloud storage.
  • Open the app on the other device and give it a minute. The first sync of a long journal with photos takes longer.

Your name, profile photo, mantras, and settings are not synced. They stay on each device.

A reminder didn't ring

  • Check that notifications are allowed. In OmniFlow, Settings → Notifications shows the current permission. If it says they are not allowed, open the Settings app, find OmniFlow, and turn notifications on.
  • A Focus mode such as Do Not Disturb or Sleep silences notifications from every app.
  • Only items marked as a Reminder ring. A plain agenda does not, unless you copy it to Apple Calendar, which adds its own alert.
  • With iCloud sync, a reminder made on one device rings on another only after that device has received it. Opening the app there makes sure it has.

My Apple Watch says "Open OmniFlow on your iPhone once to connect"

The watch gets its data from the iPhone app. Open OmniFlow on the iPhone once while the watch is nearby and today's agenda arrives. The Apple Watch app works with iPhone, not with iPad.

A widget says "Open OmniFlow to see your day"

Widgets show a copy of your agenda that the app prepares. If the app has not been opened for a couple of days, that copy is out of date, and the widget says so rather than showing an old day. Open the app once and it refreshes.

How does the journal lock work? What if Face ID doesn't recognise me?

Settings → Privacy → Lock Journal asks for Face ID or Touch ID before the Journal tab opens or a backup is exported. There is no separate OmniFlow password to forget: if Face ID fails, iOS offers your device passcode instead.

Why is Day Patterns still empty?

Patterns appear once you have logged a mood on 14 different days. They compare days with and without something, so they need both kinds of day before anything can be said.

How do the Apple Calendar, Reminders, and Health options work?

  • Calendar and Reminders (Settings → Calendar & Reminders): copies the agendas and reminders you create into Apple's apps. Turning a switch off removes the copies OmniFlow added and leaves your agendas in OmniFlow as they are.
  • Seeing your calendar in OmniFlow: tap Show my Apple Calendar events here on the Schedule tab.
  • Health (Settings → Health → Save Moods to Health): saves the mood of each new journal entry as State of Mind. OmniFlow never reads anything from Health.

How do I change the language?

OmniFlow follows your device language, in English or Bahasa Indonesia. To use a different language for this app only, open the Settings app, find OmniFlow in the list of apps, and choose Language.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Data Management → Erase All Data permanently removes every journal entry, agenda, photo, and focus session. If iCloud sync is on, it also removes them from iCloud and from your other synced devices. This cannot be undone, so export a backup first if you might want anything back.
