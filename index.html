Your day, your focus, and your journal in one place.

OmniFlow is a planner, a focus timer, and a journal that work together, for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch.

  • Free
  • No account
  • No ads
  • No tracking

Coming soon to the App Store.

Your day, in one view

Agendas, reminders, and your Apple Calendar events sit in a single list, with what's next always at the top. Routines repeat and keep their streaks.

Attention you can protect

A focus timer you can tie to the agenda you're working on. The countdown stays on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, and on your wrist.

How the week actually went

Write a few lines and pick a mood. A 30-day trend and Day Patterns show what tends to go with your lighter days.

Yours, and only yours

There is no account and no server. Everything stays on your device — and, only if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own private iCloud, which OmniFlow cannot read.
